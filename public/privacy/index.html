§1

Overview

Just One More Dance ("we," "us," "the App") is a line dancing companion: it helps you keep a personal list of dances you want to learn, are learning, and have learned; tag those dances to the venues where you dance them; browse what other dancers are tagging at venues near you; and, as a Premium feature, connect with friends to see shared activity and plan nights out.

This policy explains what information the App collects, why, and what control you have over it. It applies wherever you use Just One More Dance — iOS, Android, or the web.

§2

What we collect

Account information. When you sign up with an email address, we store that address and your authentication credentials through our database provider, Supabase.

Your dance list. The dances you mark Want to Learn, Learning Now, or Learned, any song swap you note for a dance, and the dates those entries were added or changed.

Venue activity. The venues you add to My Venues, the dances you tag to each one, and any venue you endorse with a thumbs‑up.

Friends and events. If you use the Friends feature: your friend connections and requests, dance lists you share with a friend, and any event you create or RSVP to.

Subscription status. Whether you have an active Premium subscription and which plan. Your actual payment details (card number, etc.) are collected and stored by our payment processors — RevenueCat, and depending on platform, Apple, Google, or Stripe — never by us directly.

Dance catalog searches. Search terms you enter pass through our own server to BootStepper, the third-party catalog that supplies dance step data, so that BootStepper never sees who you are.

§3

How we use it

  • To run the core features: My List, My Venues, Venues, and Friends.
  • To show you and the friends you've connected with each other's shared activity.
  • To unlock and manage your Premium subscription.
  • To power the Venues tab's "N dancers report this here" counts — these are aggregated across everyone tagging a venue; your individual tags are never shown to other users through this feature, only the total.
§4

How we share it

We share information only as needed to run the App:

  • Supabase — hosts our database and handles authentication.
  • RevenueCat, and depending on platform Apple, Google, or Stripe — process your subscription and payment.
  • BootStepper — supplies dance catalog data in response to your searches, without your account identity attached.
  • Friends you connect with — see only what the Friends feature is designed to share (their view of your list, venues, and events), never your account credentials or payment details.
We do not sell your personal information, to anyone, for any purpose.
§5

Retention

We keep your account and its associated data for as long as your account stays active. If you'd like your account and data deleted, contact us at the address below and we'll remove it, other than records we're required to keep for tax, fraud-prevention, or legal reasons.

§6

Your choices

  • Edit or remove anything on your dance list or in My Venues at any time, directly in the App.
  • Remove a friend connection whenever you like.
  • Cancel a Premium subscription anytime through your Apple or Google subscription settings (mobile) or Profile → Manage Subscription (web).
  • Request a full copy or deletion of your account data by writing to us.
§7

Children

Just One More Dance is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll remove it.

§8

Security

Your data is protected by row-level security on our database, meaning your personal entries are only ever readable by your own account, plus industry-standard encryption in transit. Payment information never touches our servers at all — it's handled entirely by our payment processors.

§9

Changes to this policy

If we make a material change, we'll update the effective date at the top of this page. Continuing to use the App after a change means you accept the update.

§10

Contact
