Coming soon

What's coming next

  • iOS app updateOn the way — profile pictures, Offline Mode, and a tab-bar polish fix, already built and headed to TestFlight next.
  • Android — bringing Just One More Dance to Android.
  • An even richer YouTube sync — a second auto-playlist just for demo videos, alongside the teach-video one that already exists.
  • Playlists split by status — two auto-playlists instead of one, "Want to Learn + Learning" and "Learned," ordered easiest to hardest.
Sept 21 – 27 Latest
  • YouTube playlist sync — a third sync provider alongside Spotify and Apple Music, for your teach/reference videos.
  • Pick exactly what syncs — All, Learning, Learned, or any combination, instead of one fixed choice.
  • Faster bulk importing with an "accept top result" option, and each sync provider now shows its own logo.
  • Three subscription tiers — Grapevine, Line Up, and Floor Boss — replacing the single Premium plan, so you can pick exactly the features you want.
  • Profile pictures — upload a photo and position it with a drag-and-pinch circular framer, so nothing important gets cropped out.
  • Offline Mode — a way out if sign-in ever gets stuck on a bad connection, so you're never just staring at a spinner.
  • Fixed a crash some people hit right after signing in on a fresh install.
  • Fixed a bug where a dance imported from a friend's list wouldn't show its video until you separately searched for it on Home.
  • Added behind-the-scenes crash reporting, so bugs get caught and fixed faster.
Sept 14 – 20
  • Playlist sync — sync your dance list to a real Spotify or Apple Music playlist, created and kept up to date automatically without undoing edits you make by hand in either app.
  • Launched the How to Use guide.
  • Raised the free plan's dance list cap from 30 to 50.
  • Real music and teach-video links now show up on dance cards, pulled straight from the catalog.
  • Added a "back to top" button and sticky action bars for long lists.
  • Profile got "fun facts" (favorite dance, dancing since, first dance), friend-list polish, and award badges for learning milestones.
  • Event scheduling now uses a real date/time picker instead of typing it in.
Sept 7 – 13
  • Added Premium subscriptions, with discount codes for early supporters.
  • Free accounts now include up to 30 dances.
  • Added an offline notepad — jot down dance names with no signal, import them once you're back online.
  • Added venue filtering.
  • App Store prep: app icon, and the Terms, Privacy, and Support pages.
Aug 31 – Sept 6
  • Friends — add people, see each other's lists, and share dances.
  • Added the My Venues tab.
  • Quick-add a dance in one tap, plus manual link editing and copy/paste import from notes.
  • Reworked the Profile page.
Aug 24 – 30
  • First build: search the full dance catalog, save dances to My List, and the first version of the Venues tab.
