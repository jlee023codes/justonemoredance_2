Contact us

For anything not covered below: justonemoredanceapp@gmail.com. Include your account email and, if it's about a specific dance or venue, its name — it helps us track down what you're seeing faster.

★

Manage or cancel Premium

Premium unlocks the Venues and Friends tabs. How you manage it depends on where you subscribed:

  • On iOS — Settings app → your name → Subscriptions, or App Store → your profile → Subscriptions.
  • On Android — Play Store → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  • On the web — in the app, go to Profile → Manage Subscription. This opens a page where you can cancel or update payment details directly.

Cancelling stops future renewals but doesn't refund the current billing period. Refunds are handled by the App Store, Play Store, or Stripe according to their own policies.

🔒

Delete my account

Email us at the address above from the account's email, or just ask, and we'll delete your account and its data — your dance list, venue tags, and friend connections — within a few days. This doesn't cancel an active subscription; do that separately through Apple, Google, or the web Manage Subscription page first, or you may continue to be billed.

👤

A dance looks wrong

Dance names, songs, counts, and step details come from BootStepper, a third-party catalog we don't operate. If something looks off, let us know and we'll pass it along — but the most reliable fix is usually to confirm with your instructor, since we can't guarantee the catalog's accuracy ourselves.

🐛

Report a bug

Email us what happened, what you expected instead, and which device/platform (iOS, Android, or web browser) you were using. A screenshot helps a lot if it's something visual.

✎

Suggest a feature

We read every email. Tell us what you're trying to do and where it fell short — that's usually more useful to us than a specific fix, since it lets us find the best solution.
